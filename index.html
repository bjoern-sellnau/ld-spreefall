Every building, street, tree and bend of the river here comes from OpenStreetMap. The renderer, the physics and the sound are written from scratch in WebGL2 and the Web Audio API, with no game engine and no 3D library.

How to walk around

Shiftrun
Spacejump onto kerbs and low walls, or leap while reflexes are on
E, or right Shiftreflexes: the city slows down and you do not; in The Hunt, E is the cloak, Shift the suit's sprint, Space held a higher jump, F takes a man in reach by the throat (the trigger held throws him, F again lets go) or punches what is too big to lift, G a grenade, B the visor, N Nano Vision, X the bow's draw weight
Mouse, or drag on the right of the screenlook
Pphoto mode, hides the interface and unlocks the clock
Rreload; with the bow, change the arrow head: fire, poison, explosive
Menlarge the map; click it to set a mark for the compass
Nsound on and off
Fget into the nearest vehicle, or out of the one you are in; a silent takedown on a guard who has not seen you; a finisher on a demon that reels
Jin Tactics and Slayer, the journal: every district, and the camps or nests given back, or given back harder
drivingW forward, S back, A D steer, Shift brake, left mouse fires. You face the way it is pointing when you get in, and the view turns with it.
flyingit flies where you look; W S throttle, A D turn, Space climbs, right mouse drops, left mouse fires
in a mechW A S D walks, the mouse aims the torso, left mouse fires what is in its hands, right mouse empties the rocket pod, Shift dashes, Space jumps and the landing opens the road
